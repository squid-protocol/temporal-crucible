# Timing pilot: what 269 commits show (EXPLORATORY; every 25th first-parent commit, 3 small corpora, 1 repeat of CPU seconds; noise floor pending)

1. Engine crash window. The engine fails on every corpus at sampled commits 100, 125 and 150 (2026-04-21, 04-22, 05-01; `TypeError: NoneType * float` inside the engine). Last good sample 75 (04-17), first good 175 (05-30): at least 50 commits, probably about ten days, where this engine could not scan these inputs. The all-corpora +13-22% step bracketing that window (04-17 -> 05-30) is uncertain.
2. Cost growth, first to last timed commit: keyword-rosetta 0.8 -> 42.9 CPU s (x52), cics-crucible 1.4 -> 12.9 (x9.5; files x2.8, so x3.4 per file), one-file-per-language control 0.3 -> 37.5 (x117; files 45 -> 52). The cost of a small many-language scan rose two orders of magnitude, almost all of it a per-language cost, not a per-file cost.
3. Events, not drift (descriptive): per corpus 7-14 of 265 consecutive-sample pairs change by more than 10%; those pairs carry 89% (keyword-rosetta), 73% (cics) and 94% (control) of the net log growth. Sub-10% pairs add x1.5-x1.8 in total. The top 10% of pairs hold 54-65% of the total absolute log change. Of the 35 steps over 10%, 28 are increases and 7 decreases. Each pair spans 25 commits, so a pair can hold several changes.
4. Where the steps are: 12 of 15 pairs in March-April, 22 of 261 in August-September, and 2 of 447 corpus-pairs in October-November. After 2026-10-02 the small-corpus cost is flat across thousands of commits; the later steps found (E2, E3, the November drift) only show on language-crucible.
5. Cross-corpus: 22 flagged brackets; 5 hit all three corpora, 3 hit two, 14 hit one (7 control-only, 6 keyword-rosetta-only, 1 cics-only). Control-only and keyword-rosetta-only steps are per-language-cost steps; cics (mainframe, few languages) moves on per-file work.
6. A cluster around 2026-09-26..28: steps at samples 2850-2875 (keyword-rosetta x1.86, control x1.6), 2875-2900 (x1.25, x1.4) and 2925-2950 (x3.42, x5.74; contains E1 #3929), plus a decrease (x0.78) in between. The 7.6x found earlier at E1 sits inside a sequence of at least four steps in three days around the lazy-regex-compilation work (#3914, #3923, #3931, #3929); E1 is the largest single step, not the only one. The refinement stage will pin the others.
7. Early history: cics x2.72 and keyword-rosetta x1.73 between 03-26 and 04-01, and control x3.86 between 04-01 and 04-17 (the package restructure period): a per-language start-up cost appears while the engine gains language definitions. Mechanism not tested.
Limits: one repeat, no noise floor yet (±5% measured earlier on a different set), 25-commit pair width, three small corpora only (stage 2 adds language-crucible), no output-parity check in the pilot.
