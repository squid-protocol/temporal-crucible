# Linux kernel `Fixes:` pairs in the scanned core subsystems (counts only; no blast-radius analysis yet)

Script `tools/sensor/kernel_fixes_count.py`, output `linux_fixes_pairs_output.txt`, pairs `links_linux_all.json` (scratch). Scans: 53 quarterly snapshots of `fs mm kernel net lib block security crypto ipc init` and the generic headers, 2014-01 to 2026-11 (76,000 functions growing to 144,000; 267,000 to 595,000 resolved call edges).

**Pairs.** 154,466 `Fixes:` trailers in the kernel history; 152,957 resolve to an earlier commit (1,096 reverts, 1,503 unresolved or self-referencing, 6 not ordered). **39,198 pairs have both commits touching a scanned `.c`/`.h` file; 34,349 of them have the inducing commit from 2014 on** (the first snapshot). Lag: median 279 days, 17% within 30 days, **45% a year or more**. In **3,702 (11%) the fix and the inducing commit share no file**: the cross-file pairs, the case where a file-level view cannot see the connection (compare Lyu et al.: over 13% of inducing commits share no file with their fix).

**By subsystem of the fix** (the 2-level directory holding most of its changed files; pairs, of them cross-file): kernel 4,987 (473); mm 2,782 (343); include/linux 2,647 (318); net/ipv4 1,459 (176); net/core 1,215 (118); net/netfilter 1,196 (125); fs (core) 1,062 (97); fs/btrfs 1,055 (101); block 995 (113); net/sched 864 (100); include/net 844 (112); fs/xfs 735 (52); net/ipv6 734 (70); **fs/smb 725 (370, half cross-file)**; fs/nfs 605 (47); net/bluetooth 602 (32); lib 590 (66); security 582 (53); fs/f2fs 534 (35); net/mptcp 531 (45); net/mac80211 523 (38); fs/nfsd 397 (29); crypto 381 (46); fs/ext4 380 (31). Inducing commits per year: 1,308 (2014) rising to about 3,000 a year from 2016 to 2021 and 2,500 to 2,800 since.

**Scale against what we have.** Curl gave 507 usable pairs and OpenSSL and FreeBSD 177 and 103; about half of named pairs map to functions in a scan, so the kernel offers on the order of **17,000 usable pairs, about 8,000 with a fixed function outside the changed set, and about 1,800 cross-file pairs**: tens of times more, enough for tight intervals per subsystem and for the cross-file and lag questions on their own.

**Not yet.** No distance has been computed. Before running: (1) the null in `ripple_links.analyse` is sampled (200 draws with a list scan per draw) and would take days at this size; an exact version (as in `ripple_forward.py`) is needed; (2) pairs touching drivers (outside the scan) are cut by the "both commits in the scanned set" rule, so findings cover the core subsystems; (3) the hypotheses to fix beforehand: the radius replicates with a tight interval; the cross-file pairs show a radius (the ghost-commit question); callbacks (function-pointer tables, kernel `..._ops`) matter more than in curl (graph with tree-sitter references against calls only); the fade with lag is smooth and subsystem-dependent.
