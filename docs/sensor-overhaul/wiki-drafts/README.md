## Not changed here, needs your decision or a check
* **`docs/vectors.md`** says `hist_stability` and `hist_churn` are "ablated to zero on every recorded row" because every scan sets `GITGALAXY_DISABLE_GIT_HISTORY`. The v2.12.0 population database has non-zero churn on 21% and stability on 36% of sampled files (243 and 488 of 580 repositories). Either those scans ran with history on, or the statement is out of date; it should be checked and the paragraph updated. The same file's `debt_markers` entry (REWORK flagged for the density-denominator artifact) is consistent with this work: the recommended direction there (count-based, not density) is what the debt ledger does.
* **`01-09-the-continuous-delta-paradigm.md`** ("Logarithmic Churn... relative to the square root of a file's age") should get the same "age means time since last modification" wording.
* **`03-04-claim-4-comparing-languages.md`** shows ridge plots of "Volatility Exposure (Churn)" and "Instability Exposure (Age)" by language. Their data source should be checked: in scans without history both values are neutral constants, which would flatten the plots.
* **Next pages worth the same treatment:** `08-13-graveyard-detector` and `08-16-state-flux-exposure` (one keyword family each; `safety_score` is mostly mutation density), `08-04-ownership-entropy` (activity gating). Findings are in `docs/sensor-overhaul/GUIDE.md` section 3.

## What these pages do and do not claim
They state what the code does, what was measured (13 repositories, a 580-repository population sample for the descriptive results, 7 repositories for history-aware outcome tests) and what is proposed. They make **no risk or defect claim**; outcome relations are reported as small and descriptive. Third-party tools are not copied: the design notes use our own vocabulary and an open standard (SARIF) where a convention was needed (`GUIDE.md` section 4.1).
