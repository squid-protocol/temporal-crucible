Hypotheses, each judged per language stratum (C, Go, Java) and overall, primary def B:
 H1: orchestrators (fan-in<8, fan-out>=8) lift > 1. PASS if the random-effects pooled CI excludes 1 in the stratum.
 H1b: same with a per-repo percentile definition (fan-out >= p90 of fan-out, fan-in < p90 of fan-in); must agree in sign with H1.
 H3: fan-out partial Spearman (controls LOC, complexity) > 0 in a majority of repos in the stratum.
 H4: consumer-ish (in/(in+out) < 0.2, >=3 edges) lift > producer-ish (> 0.8) lift, pooled CIs non-overlapping or consumer CI above 1 and producer CI below.
 H5 (fix radius): the share of co-changed function pairs (same fix commit, <=12 functions) within 2 call hops exceeds a DIRECTORY-MATCHED null
     (b replaced by a random fix-touched function in b's directory, same file when the pair is same-file) by >= 1.5x in a majority of repos with >= 100 pairs.
Also reported, no hypothesis: utility and hub quadrants; heterogeneity (I-squared) of the orchestrator lift.
Dropped (not re-tested): lifecycle-named functions (retracted: churn effect); file-level producer ratio (no signal).
